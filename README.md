## 🔒 Your progress stays private
 
Everything is saved **in your own browser** 💾.
 
- 🙈 Nobody else can see your results, including the site owner.
- 🗑️ Your data stays until you press **Delete history** or **Reset answers**, or clear your browser data.
- 📤 **Export JSON** backs up your results, and 📥 **Import JSON** moves them to another device.

## 🚀 How to run it
 
**💻 Offline:** download `CSP11_Prep_Hub.html` and open it in any browser. No internet needed.
 
**🌐 Online:** upload the files in this repository to any static web host.

## 📅 Suggested daily routine (about 2 hours)
 
| ⏰ Time | 📌 Task |
|---|---|
| 45 min | 📖 Read one topic in Study Notes |
| 20 min | ✏️ Answer its 10 questions without looking at the answers |
| 15 min | 🔍 Read every explanation and log your mistakes |
| 40 min | ⏱️ Do a 25-question timed drill |
 
🎯 **Target:** 80%+ on every domain, and 85%+ on calculations.
